=== SUMMARY OF DOMAIN OWNERSHIP FINDINGS ===
- Manufacturing defines its own core domain entities: products (catalog master + manufacturing extensions), machines, demands, orders, plans, entries, QC plans/entries, assembly plans/entries, dispatch entries, coverage analytics, stage/workflow governance, inventory/ledger tracking.
- Manufacturing does NOT define its own supplier/customer/party master; it relies on external domain entities (likely Parties / Procurement / external supply). Manufacturing's DailyOrders/PreOrders reference demand/customer concepts but do not declare a customer master.
- Manufacturing's Products module extends the `products` table (catalog master) extensively; this indicates Manufacturing treats product as a shared master with Manufacturing-specific extensions. This is the strongest shared-candidate evidence: Product catalog master could become Shared App (already considered by user direction — Parties + Products/Items discussion). Manufacturing's extension fields (`production_source`, `requires_qc`, `cycle_time`, etc.) are Manufacturing-specific and should stay Manufacturing Suite.
- Manufacturing's MaterialManagement references `materials`, `part_material_map`, `material_ledger` (declared required_tables). These are Manufacturing domain but could overlap with a cross-suite inventory/supply system. Without external inventory module evidence, these remain Manufacturing-specific.
